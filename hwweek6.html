Vector Emotive Eye: Contempt



My vector emotive eye is inspired by the character Yzma, the villain from the show The Emperor's New Groove. She was one of my favorite cartoon characters growing up. One of the reasons I loved her so much was because of how expressive her face and eyes were. Yzma's heavy, bored eyes often showed her contempt for those she considered beneath her... which was pretty much everyone.




I had made a first version of the eye, but I felt it was not emotive enough, so I decided to keep working on it.

Reflection 2

Giuliana's Reflection - Homework #2

I enjoyed taking these pictures and experimenting with shutter speed, f/stop, and ISO. I hadn’t done it in a while, so the online class and lab were very helpful. I am glad to have taken the pictures right after listening to these lectures. I still want to practice shooting in low light more, as I think playing with colored lights and different shutter speeds can create some interesting photos.
Working with Photoshop is still my biggest challenge when it comes to photography, but with the LinkedIn Learning tutorials, it was much easier to figure some things out. I still feel like I need to rewatch them a few times, as there are some concepts that I am still struggling with such as masking, and coloring. The basic edits for this homework worked out ok, but I will continue to practice with shooting in different color profiles and choosing the best file types for the work I need to achieve, as I think the quality of my pictures could be improved.
In the future, I would like to experiment with different grid layouts, to achieve more interesting compositions, and take more portraits as those are the type of pictures I like to take and would like to learn to edit them more professionally. Also, now that I understand nondestructive editing better, I am excited to experiment with creative filters, and make stylized artistic images and collages.

Reflection 3

I liked playing with depth of field and different focal points. In general, I gravitate towards shallow depth of field pictures, as I think the contrast between the subject and background creates a very aesthetically pleasing image and helps the subject stand out. Therefore, for a picture like the ones I took for this assignment, or portraits, I would use shallow depth of field. However, I am glad I got to practice achieving a deep depth of field, and learned the limits of my camera's ISO. I can see myself using this technique for landscapes, photos with multiple subjects that are positioned at different distances from the lens, and in my filmmaking.
